Privacy and retention
What is sent: your current command and only the company-scoped records selected by registered tools.
What EZ360PM stores: operational metadata and, when enabled below, redacted length-limited summaries. Full prompts and full model responses are not stored in the EZ360PM database.
Pilot feedback: helpful/not-helpful comments and incident details are stored only when a user deliberately submits them for review.
What is never available to the model: database access, company selection, public document tokens, Stripe secrets, refunds, or unrestricted email/financial tools.
